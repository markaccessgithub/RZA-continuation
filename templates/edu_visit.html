Riget Zoo Adventures

Education lies at the heart of all that we do, and a trip to Banham Zoo offers an unmissable opportunity to take learning outside of the classroom.
From Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) to higher education and beyond, our group visits provide immersive experiences that ignite curiosity and foster a deep appreciation for animals and the environment. Through interactive exhibits, engaging presentations, and hands-on activities, we aim to inspire students of all ages to explore and connect with the wonders of the natural world.


To book a school trip or if you have any additional questions please contact our education department at RZAschools@gmail.com.
